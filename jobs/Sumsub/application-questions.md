# Sumsub - Application Questions

## Q1: Are you ready to work under a Service Agreement (B2B Agreement)?

This means you'd be a freelance contractor, not a permanent employee. You invoice them, handle your own taxes and social contributions.

**Suggested answer:** Yes

If you don't have a company/VAT number yet, you can add: "Yes, I'm comfortable with B2B contracting and will arrange the necessary legal setup."

## Q2: Please share your compensation expectations in EUR, gross (before taxes)

**Suggested answer:** EUR 85,000 - 95,000 gross per year

Rationale: Senior but transitioning into this role type. For a remote EMEA role and 3+ years requirement, this is competitive without pricing yourself out against the other 2 applicants.
