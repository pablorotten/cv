# 🛣️ THE ROAD — the picture in your head

> **This is NOT a to-do list. It's a MAP.**
> Draw it on the giant 45cm post-it. One road. You are a person walking it.
> Blank cells get filled in **Monday**, when the assessment lands.
> If you ever freeze → look up at the wall → find your dot → take one step.

---

## 🎨 How to draw it on the post-it

- Draw it **left → right** as a **road / path**, not a grid of boxes.
- A little **🚶 dot** marks where you are. Move it every day.
- Each checkpoint gets a **📌 sticky** = that day's visible win. **Peel it off when done.**
  (Peeling a sticky = your missing "checkbox." That's the dopamine. That's the whole trick.)
- Leave the `{ ____ }` cells **empty** today. Terrain now, details Monday.

## 🧭 Daily habit (30 seconds)

1. 🚶 Put your dot where the truth is. (No lying to the map.)
2. Ask: *what's the ONE thing I can finish today so there's a 📌?*
3. When it's done → **peel the sticky**. Physically. Then stop for the day.

## 🥶 Freeze protocol

You drift to "stupid things" → that's the signal: the current step **lost its picture**.
Don't push with willpower. **Look at the wall.** Find your dot. Re-draw just that ONE step
until it's an image again. The freeze is a missing picture, never laziness.
