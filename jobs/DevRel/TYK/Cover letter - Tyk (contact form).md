Hello Tyk team,

I ran Tyk in production for five years at N-SIDE, and I want to be honest about how I used it. We needed a public API in front of a mature Scala backend with a slow release cycle, we hired someone who knew APIs but not Scala, and we ran one AWS instance per customer so licensing cost mattered. Tyk was the right call on cost and on speed. It was also the wrong tool for part of the job, and I did not know why at the time. I have since worked out what a gateway is for versus where application logic belongs, and I recorded a video about it: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8GkpmMJqsI

That misjudgement is why I want this job. I wrote all the external documentation for that API, made the onboarding screencasts, and handled the technical mail it generated. Reducing that support load was part of the job, not a side effect. So the parts of this role I would be weakest at, speaking at meetups and owning channels, are the parts I have been training for on my own, and the part I am strongest at, explaining how an API product actually behaves in a real company, is the part I have already been doing unpaid.

A few other things. I built roughly 50 endpoints on that gateway and onboarded five new customers through it. I am at 10+ years of engineering and I am comfortable saying the junior title is a step down on paper, but I would rather grow into DevRel from real production experience than the other way round. I use AI daily and I do care about the difference between AI-assisted and AI slop. And I will happily tell the story of how we used a gateway as a BFF in front of anyone who asks.
