### The core problem we're solving

Millions of people and AI agents around the world are working with the same thing right now: text documents. Humans interact with them through a rich text editor in the browser. Agents interact with them through APIs and structured data like JSON.

While the vast majority of users have no idea about the underlying technology, and even most web-savvy people think of a text editor as a simple piece of HTML, most of the content on the web comes through them.

And while it always seems like an easy problem to solve for web developers at first, it's extremely difficult and finicky to do well, even in the age of agentic coding and especially when you need to build more sophisticated agentic document workflows or editor UIs like Google Docs or Notion do.

That's why we built Tiptap. Transforming this complexity into fun.

### About Tiptap

Chances are high that you've already used Tiptap without knowing it in one of the apps you have open daily. With 37k GitHub stars and over 45M npm downloads per month, Tiptap is the most popular open-source editor framework worldwide, used by tens of thousands of businesses such as Anthropic, Spotify, and GitLab to build advanced content editing experiences into their apps.
