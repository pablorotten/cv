# Unframe - Application Questions

## Q1: Can you talk me through your experience in enterprise solution engineering, particularly in complex technical sales environments?

### Suggested Answer

While my title has been Software Engineer, the role at N-SIDE (2021-2026) was a hybrid between engineering and solutions. Our platform was a complex SaaS for clinical trial optimization sold to pharma enterprises -- multi-stakeholder sales with technical validation cycles.

**Technical Discovery & Demos:** I was the technical bridge between end-users (clinical trial analysts) and core algorithm experts. I led discovery conversations to understand their simulation pain points, then designed and delivered solutions. One example: I productized a REST API via TYK gateway by consolidating scattered middleware logic into a single endpoint, which I then demonstrated to stakeholders to show performance gains.

**Objection Handling & Validation:** I served as primary technical contact for 70% of user-reported tickets. Instead of just fixing bugs, I translated complex simulation issues into Confluence troubleshooting guides and visual diagrams, accelerating resolution and reducing repeat questions.

**Training & Enablement:** I conducted live training sessions for 50+ consultants to migrate them onto a new web workflow, improving data entry speed up to 100x. This is directly analogous to post-sale enablement and onboarding.

**Proof of Value / Automation:** I designed Argo workflows that automated over 50% of monthly client data extraction requests and accelerated QA/UAT deployments by 60-70%, eliminating manual operations. These were essentially proof-of-value engagements showing customers what automation could do for them.

**Cross-functional Influence:** I served as Kanban Lead, ran standups, and channeled customer feedback into product decisions -- the same feedback loop between customers, Sales, and Product that SEs manage.

I don't have a formal "Solutions Engineer" title, but I've been doing the work for 5+ years: technical discovery, demos, objection handling, training, documentation, and customer advocacy.
